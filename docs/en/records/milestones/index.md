# Milestone Reviews

Recorded in units of "milestones": once a relatively complete stretch of time has passed, look back and write a summary.

A milestone is longer than a month but shorter than a semester, and it should cover somewhat weightier things than the weekly and monthly logs — what you did, what you got, what pitfalls you hit, and where to head next.
