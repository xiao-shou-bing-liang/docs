# Monthly Notes

Recorded in units of "months": wrap up the things you did and the things you built this month.

Weekly logs are fragmentary and monthly logs are complete. Usually at the end of the month you flip back through the month's weekly logs, pull out what is worth keeping, and while you are at it decide what to do next month.

> [!tip] How to Write
>
> One sentence summing up the month's theme, then three sections — "Did", "Learned", "Next Month" — is enough.
