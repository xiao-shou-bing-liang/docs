The download speed is fine here in China to begin with. The extensions below can all be searched for directly in the Extensions view

### ode-DOSBox

Provides an interface for running DOSBox and its variants inside VSCode

### MASM/TASM

Enables quick invocation of assemblers such as DOSBox. It mainly targets single-file assembly language learning under DOS, and may suit courses like *Assembly Language* and *Microcomputer Principles*
