### Physical Training

- Running

  Two months have passed. I haven't managed to run every single day, but I'm running far more often than last semester. The biggest problem is that Guilin flooded this year — it rained nonstop and the rain was enormous. Reports later said it was the biggest flood peak since 1998, and the whole downtown area was basically underwater, but our campus sits up on a hill so it wasn't much of a problem. As for my running level, I can get my 3 km into the 4-minute range now; my fastest time was at a 4:52 pace. And then, unexpectedly, I can still run 1 km at 3:50 with nobody pacing me — honestly that was a surprise. My plan going forward is to train properly, aim for a sub-5:00 pace over 5 km, then a 4:00 pace over 3 km, and so on.

- Strength

  Ever since I banged my knee a little I've only trained legs once. I have to say that training legs really is a rather painful process. My squat PR is now 140 kg — a weight I couldn't even budge in a half squat before — and now I can full-squat it, so I'm already much stronger than before. As for the deadlift, about 50 days ago my lower back felt awful during pulls so I started fixing my form; once I had the form right, my max was basically 140 kg as well. The reason my max is low is mainly that I still train the deadlift too little and never drew up a detailed training plan, so my deadlift lags behind my squat. On top of that my grip strength is insufficient — no matter how heavy the bar, my hands can't hold it. The bench press is where I've progressed the most this semester: from pressing 50 kg with sore wrists and finding it a real struggle at the start, to pressing 100 kg with almost no sticking point now, where 50 kg is like the weight of drinking water. Still, I need to keep accumulating.
