## Training

Because I've basically put my focus here, I've made some breakthroughs

As for running, a 7 km at a 6:00 min/km pace is no big problem now, but that's still far from enough. Next year at the school sports meet I plan to run 10 km (conditions permitting), so next I need to put my focus on aerobic work. The target is 75 kg, aiming at next year's Liuzhou Marathon and Guilin Marathon, and I'll try to finish at a 5:00 min/km pace

Strength has gone up quite a bit

- Flat barbell bench press went from a single rep at 100 kg at the end of June to 100 kg for 5*5 now, with a PR of 110 kg. Dumbbell incline bench press went from not being familiar with the movement pattern at all to 30 kg sets with no pressure, and the other accessory movements are all progressing steadily

- Shoulder press went from barely managing sets at 20 kg to barely managing sets at 30 kg now; for lateral raises, flying 10 kg or 12.5 kg is no problem; and for rear delts on the pec-deck machine I can now put in the 45 kg plate
- As for arms, my triceps are much stronger now, but my biceps are so-so—basically little change compared with when I first started training—and forearm size has improved slightly as well
- Squats have regressed badly, mainly because last semester a deadlift barbell hit my knee and I stopped training for a while, plus recently going up to a 155 kg deadlift caused one-sided pain just above my tailbone, so I haven't really resumed squat training this semester. I only just did a recovery session yesterday, and I'll gradually build back up, then work on polishing my form. Deadlift feels like it's improving, because Chen Anping has been coaching my form; a few days ago I pulled 150 kg instantly, and I have to say that although this movement still needs polishing, at least my lower back no longer compensates and gets strained
- Back has improved a little too—at least on a good day I can do 5 pull-ups. In my view the back is the hardest part to train; I haven't yet gone to learn from George, and going forward I need to put more focus on legs and back.
